
# HealthSet

## Overview
`UHealthSet` defines attributes related to health, such as current health, max health, healing, and damage.

## Attributes
- **Health**: Current health value.
- **MaxHealth**: Maximum health value.
- **Healing**: Incoming healing value.
- **Damage**: Incoming damage value.

## Events
- **OnHealthChanged**: Triggered when health changes.
- **OnMaxHealthChanged**: Triggered when max health changes.
- **OnOutOfHealth**: Triggered when health reaches zero.
